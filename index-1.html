Frequently asked questions

Short answers for students

Is ShiftEduTech only an IT course institute?

Courses are how you enter. The product is a six-step path: learn, practice, build, get mentored, become job-ready, and get career support.

Do you guarantee a job?

No. Placement assistance includes mock interviews, resume help, and counselor follow-up.

Which courses does ShiftEduTech offer?

Java Full Stack, Python Full Stack, .NET, UI/UX, Data Science, AI, Business Analytics, AWS, Cyber Security, VLSI, Embedded Systems & IoT, and ChatGPT & AI Tools.

Where are the campuses?

ShiftEduTech has campuses in Kondapur, Hyderabad and 1-Town, Eluru.

How do I enquire about a batch?

Use the enquiry form above or contact the admissions numbers shown on this page.

Do you offer internships and projects?

Yes. Internships, industrial training, and mini or major projects are part of the career path.
